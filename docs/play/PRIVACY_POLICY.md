# Angle Lens privacy policy (draft)

_Last updated: [date of publication]_ · _Developer: [owner name] · Contact: [owner contact email]_

> Draft based on the 1.0.0-rc1 code. Publish it at a public URL and link it in Play Console. Update it
> before any release that adds network features (for example, AI assist).

Angle Lens measures angles in images on your device. It does not collect, store on a server, sell or share personal data.

**Camera.** Angle Lens uses the camera only while the measuring screen is open and the camera is started. Frames are processed in your device's memory to show the live view and to measure. They are not uploaded and are not saved unless you choose to save an image. The camera is released when the app goes to the background.

**Photos you open.** When you use Open Photo, you pick an image with the system picker. Angle Lens reads it on the device to measure it. It is not uploaded or copied elsewhere.

**Images you save.** When you save a measurement, Angle Lens creates an annotated image in its private cache and opens the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes (for example Photos, Files or another app). Anything you send to another app is handled under that app's policy.

**Network.** This version makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or account services.

**Storage and backup.** The app keeps no measurement history or settings between sessions. Android cloud backup of app data is disabled.

**Children.** Angle Lens is a general-purpose tool and is not directed at children.

**Changes.** If data handling changes in a future version, this policy and the app's Play Store data-safety section will be updated before that version is released.
